THE JOURNEY

Some trips become memories.

I had visited Srisailam before, but this trip felt different. The people, the road, the rain, the darshan and all the unexpected little moments came together perfectly.

01

The plan

My friends had been planning this trip for almost a month, but we never found the right time. Finally, we decided to go. At the last minute, work almost made me cancel. I changed my mind — and that is how our Srisailam trip finally began.

02

4:30 AM — The journey begins

We started around 4:30 AM with a prayer and a coconut break. After an hour, we noticed the car headlights were not working properly, so we drove carefully. By 7:00 AM we reached Kalwakurthy, had tea, filled the petrol and continued with devotional songs playing.

03

Pathala Ganga

Around 9:00 AM we reached the Srisailam area and later visited Pathala Ganga. We walked down around 600 steps to reach the water. Going down was easy; coming back up was a completely different story! We were exhausted, but it became one of the best memories.

04

The darshan

At around 3:00 PM, we went to the temple in traditional attire, expecting a one or two-hour wait. Surprisingly, our darshan was completed within 10 minutes. We had prasadam and took memorable pictures in front of the temple.

THE MOMENTS

Little moments, big memories.

Food, rain, hostel-like memories and one unexpected tyre problem.

05

Rain & hostel memories

After dinner, the surroundings reminded us of our hostel days. We sat together in the corridor, watched the rain and talked for a while before going to sleep.

06

The tyre surprise

The next morning, after another darshan, we discovered that the front-left tyre had no air. After about 30 minutes of searching, a mechanic filled it. Problem solved — another story added.

07

The journey back

We had breakfast and drove through the beautiful ghat roads. Around noon, my friends dropped me at my office because I had a 1:00 PM shift.

Back to reality.A tired body, but a mind full of memories.
