THE STORY

Some trips become memories.

I had visited the famous Srisailam Temple twice before, but I had never experienced it the way I did this time. The journey, the people, the unexpected moments, the rain and the darshan all came together to make this trip special.

01

It all started with a plan

My friends had been planning this trip for almost a month, but somehow we never found the right time. Finally, we decided to go. Then, at the last minute, my work commitments almost cancelled everything.

My friends were disappointed and even decided not to go without me. Later, I changed my mind. I didn't want the whole plan to collapse because of me, so I said, “Okay, let's go!”

“

Sometimes the trips that almost don't happen become the ones you remember the most.

THE CREWFive friends, one unforgettable trip.
02

4:30 AM — The journey begins

We started around 4:30 AM. Before leaving, we broke a coconut and prayed that everything would go well.

After about an hour, we discovered that the car headlights were not working properly. Since it was still dark, we had to slow down and drive carefully.

By 7:00 AM we reached Kalwakurthy, stopped for tea, filled the car with fuel and continued our journey with Lord Shiva's devotional songs playing in the background.

07:00 AMTea break at Kalwakurthy
03

Srisailam Dam & the ghats

Around 9:00 AM, we reached the beautiful Srisailam Dam. The greenery, hills and winding ghat roads made the journey feel like a destination in itself.

PATHALA GANGABeautiful water, hills and a lot of steps.
04

The search for a room

We reached Srisailam around 10:00 AM, tired after starting so early. Our next challenge was finding accommodation.

After searching different places, all four of us finally found Gouda Satram. The room cost around ₹1,200 for 24 hours.

STAY Gouda Satram

₹1,200 / 24 hours

05

Pathala Ganga — 600 steps!

We went to Pathala Ganga to freshen up. The place is reached by around 600 steps, and while going down it felt manageable.

Coming back up was a completely different story! 😂 The hard stairs left us exhausted, but it became one of the memorable moments of the trip.

600 steps down… and then back up! 😅
06

THE MOMENT

Darshan in just 10 minutes.

We expected one or two hours because it was a weekend. We stood in line expecting a long wait. But surprisingly, our darshan was completed within just 10 minutes.

It was peaceful, beautiful and completely unexpected — a moment we will never forget.

07

Exploring Srisailam

After darshan, we had prasadam and took some memorable pictures before exploring a few more places.

08

Food, rain & hostel memories

By 7:00 PM everyone was tired and hungry, so we went for idlis and punugulu. Later, around 8:30 PM, we heard the dinner bell.

The surroundings reminded us of our hostel days — the building, the peaceful atmosphere and sitting together in the corridor.

Then it started raining heavily. We sat there enjoying the rain and chatting before finally going to sleep.

🌧️

09

The tyre surprise 😅

The next morning, three of my friends woke up around 3:30 AM and went for another darshan. I stayed back because I was exhausted.

When we were ready to leave, we discovered that the front-left tyre had no air. We weren't sure whether it was punctured or simply had lost air.

After about 30 minutes of searching, my friend found a mechanic who filled the tyre with air. Problem solved!

BOOM! 0 PSI vibes 😅 One more story to tell.

THE WAY BACK

Back to reality.

We had breakfast and drove back through the beautiful ghat roads. Around 12:00 PM, my friends dropped me at my office because I had an afternoon shift at 1:00 PM.

I went straight to work with a tired body — but a mind full of memories.

And thanks to Shashi and Shiva for making this trip even more beautiful and memorable.
